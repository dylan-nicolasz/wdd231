NP Membership Benefits

Designed for registered non-profit and community organizations.

  • Directory listing in the business directory.
  • Access to chamber networking events and mixers.
  • Community bulletin access.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Ideal for solo entrepreneurs and starting small businesses.

  • All NP Tier benefits.
  • 10% discount on chamber-hosted training seminars.
  • Social media welcome highlight upon joining.

Silver Membership Benefits

For growing companies needing stronger marketing exposure.

  • All Bronze Tier benefits.
  • Eligible for Home Page Spotlight rotation.
  • 25% discount on event booths and sponsorship opportunities.
  • Access to quarterly business roundtable discussions.

Gold Membership Benefits

Premium tier offering maximum visibility and influence in Quito.

  • All Silver Tier benefits.
  • Priority placement on Home Page Business Spotlights.
  • Free annual booth at the Quito Business Expo.
  • Direct sponsorship placement in quarterly email newsletters.
  • VIP invitation to Executive Board strategy dinners.
